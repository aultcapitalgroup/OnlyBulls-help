OnlyBulls Rips is a digital experience where real, physical Pokémon, One Piece and Sports trading cards are "ripped" to reveal a single card. That card is then added to your wallet and you can sell it back, keep it in your OnlyBulls wallet or ship it to you (Not yet available).

## What is a rip

A rip is how you open a card pack in OnlyBulls. You pay the pack price, the pack is opened, and one card is revealed.

The reveal is a digital collectible in your OnlyBulls wallet. It represents a real, physical card that is vaulted by Collector Crypt. Collector Crypt sets the odds and draws the card when the pack is opened.

After the reveal, you can **Keep** the card in your OnlyBulls wallet or **Sell** it back through a buyback offer. Shipping the physical card to you is not yet available.

## What is in a Pack

Each pack contains either a Pokémon, One Piece or Sports trading card of varying rarities. One card per pack.

## Where to find Rips

You can start a rip from two places in the app:

- **Wallet** tab: Under **Your Collection**, tap the **Rip your first pack** card, then tap **Rip a pack**.
- **Explore** tab: In the **Rips** row, tap **View all**.

On the Rips screen, use the **All**, **Pokemon**, **One Piece**, and **Sports** tabs to browse packs. Tap a pack to open its pack page.

## Before you rip

- Pay with **USDC** on the **Base** network from your OnlyBulls wallet. If you do not have enough USDC, the app will prompt you to get more. See [Add money to your wallet](/wallet/add-money).
- You can set a daily spending cap or pause new purchases in [Responsible play](#responsible-play). These controls limit or pause new pack purchases only. Your existing orders, collection and buyback remain available.

## Pack odds

Odds vary by pack. Check the odds for the pack you are about to open in the app:

1. Open the pack page.
2. Open **What's Inside**.
3. Tap the info icon to open **Pack odds**.

**What's Inside** shows **Recent wins** and the card pool, which you can filter by **All**, **Epic**, **Rare**, **Uncommon**, and **Common**.

**Pack odds** shows **Odds by rarity** (how often cards in each rarity tier are drawn), often as **About X in every 100 draws**, how many cards are left in the pool, and an insured value range. The insured value range is an estimate. The app shows odds by rarity tier, not for a specific card.

A percentage is the chance of that stated outcome on a **single** rip. For example, a hypothetical 1% probability means about 1 chance in 100 on that rip. It is not a promise that the outcome will happen within 100 rips.

- **Rarity is not value:** Rarity shows how often a card is drawn, not what it is worth.
- **How the draw works:** Collector Crypt sets the odds and draws your card when the pack is opened. Card counts show what is left in the pool.
- **Rare cards are not guaranteed:** Every pack is a separate draw. Opening more packs does not guarantee a rare card.
- Values are insured estimates from Collector Crypt, not guaranteed resale prices. Card value can be higher or lower than the price.

## Prices, fees and value

Each pack page shows the pack price and these figures:

- **Min value:** the low end of the insured value estimates shown for that pack.
- **Max rip:** the high end of the insured value estimates shown for that pack.
- **Buyback %:** the buyback rate shown for that pack.

These figures vary by pack. For example, a \$50 pack may show a **Min value** of \$25, a **Max rip** of \$2,501, and **Buyback** of 90%. Always check the pack page for the current numbers before you confirm.

A card's value is an estimate. It can be lower or higher than the cost of the rip, and it can change. A rip is not a promise of a return.

## Responsible play

You can open Responsible play from the pack page.

These controls limit or pause new pack purchases. Your existing orders, collection and buyback remain available.

### Daily spending cap

You can set a daily spending cap for new pack purchases.

Enter a new cap and tap **Save cap** to apply it.

### Location and age

- **Your location will be checked before you sign.** Use the **Check location** button to run this check.
- **Your age declaration is saved.** 

### Pause new purchases

You can pause new pack purchases for:

- **24-hour cooling-off period**
- **7-day self-exclusion**
- **30-day self-exclusion**
- **Indefinite self-exclusion**

Select one of the options and tap **Review pause** then **Confirm pause.**

## Troubleshooting

**I don't have enough USDC.** If you try to rip a pack without enough USDC, you will be prompted to get more USDC in the app. See [Add money to your wallet](/wallet/add-money).

**I sent funds on the wrong network.** Packs are paid in USDC on Base. Funds sent on a different network can't be used to purchase packs. 

**A pack is unavailable.** A pack may be sold out or temporarily unavailable. Try another pack, or check back later and review the current pack details in the app.

**My payment is pending or failed.** If a payment is pending, wait and check your wallet and collection before trying again. If a payment fails, you can try again when you are ready. 

**The reveal or card is delayed.** If the pack or card does not appear right away, wait and check **Your Collection** in the **Wallet** tab before starting another rip for the same purchase. If it still does not appear, contact support with the details listed below. 

**The app closed during a rip.** Reopen the app and check **Your Collection** and your wallet for the pack or card. 

**My buyback offer expired.** A buyback offer is only available until it expires. If it has expired, that offer is no longer available. You can keep the card in your collection. {/* TODO: confirm whether a new buyback offer can be requested after expiry, and on what terms */}
