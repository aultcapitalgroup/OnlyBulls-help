## Daily briefs

Briefs are short, AI-written market summaries with a crypto focus. They cover major coins, the biggest movers, tokenized stocks, interest rates and the dollar, the economic calendar, and the day's most important news.

There are two editions every day, including weekends:

| Brief | Available after |
| --- | --- |
| **Morning Brief** | 9:00 AM New York time |
| **Evening Brief** | 4:30 PM New York time |

### Where to find them

- **iOS and Android:** **Explore → More to Explore → Briefs**, or tap a brief notification.
- **Web:** the **Market update** card on Home. Click **Read**, **Listen**, or **View all**.

The **Briefs** screen shows **Today** at the top and **Past Briefs** underneath. Inside a brief, tap **See previous briefs** to go back further.

### What's in a brief

- **Quick highlights**
- **Movers behind the story**: the assets driving the day
- The full write-up
- **What this brief is built on**: the market news, economic news, and top gainers and losers it used

Tap thumbs up or thumbs down at the end to tell us how we did.

### Personalized morning briefs

If you've agreed to AI features, your morning brief may be tailored to the tokens in your [watchlists](/markets/watchlists) and wallet. The evening brief is the same for everyone.

## Listen to a brief

Tap the play icon on a brief to hear it as a short two-host audio show. A mini player appears above the tab bar with play, pause, and close buttons. On mobile, audio keeps playing when you switch apps or your phone is on silent.

## Brief notifications

You can get a notification when your morning brief is ready. To turn brief notifications off, go to **Profile → Notifications → Daily Briefs**.

## Weekly recap

Every Monday morning (New York time), a **Weekly recap** card appears at the top of **Briefs**. It looks back at the previous Monday-to-Monday week.

It covers the coins in your watchlists and wallet, or Bitcoin and Ethereum if you don't have any yet. Your recap shows each coin's low and high and how it did against Bitcoin. It also explains why it's included, for example **On your watchlist** or **One of your top holdings**, and links to news about the move. **Spot portfolio** compares your wallet value this Monday with the previous Monday. That figure includes deposits and withdrawals, so it isn't your investment return.

<Note>
Briefs and recaps are generated by AI from market data. They're for information only and aren't financial advice.
</Note>
