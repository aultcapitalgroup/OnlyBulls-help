## AI insights

Look for the **Try Agent Intelligence** button around the app. It writes a quick AI analysis of what you're looking at:

| Where | What you get |
| --- | --- |
| A news article | What the story means, with a **Bullish**, **Bearish**, or **Neutral** read and the stocks it may affect |
| A watchlist | How the tokens in your list are doing and why |
| **Market Movers** | What's behind today's biggest moves |
| **Economy** | A summary of the latest economic data |

The insight opens in a panel labelled **OnlyBulls Intelligence**. You can share it, rate it with thumbs up or down, and scroll down to **Past Insights**.

On an asset's page, **Try Agent Intelligence** works differently: it opens [AI Chat](/ai/ai-chat) with a question about that asset ready to send.

Insights need AI features turned on.

<Warning>
AI insights can be wrong and aren't financial advice.
</Warning>

## Market Pulse

**Market Pulse** is a midday snapshot of your watchlists. It's sent at about **12:00 PM New York time on US trading days**, as a notification titled **Mid-day Market Pulse**. It tells you how your watchlists are doing and names the top mover.

Tap it to see:

- **Your watchlists** at the time of the snapshot, and the top mover
- **Your watchlists now**, with live prices

You'll only get Market Pulse if your watchlists contain tokens with live prices. To turn it off, go to **Profile → Notifications → Market Pulse**.
