<Note>
**Rolling out**

Savings is being rolled out gradually. If you don't see **Save** in the More menu, it isn't available on your account yet.
</Note>

**Savings** earns interest on your Cash. Your USDC on Base goes into a yield vault that lends it out to earn interest.

- **Grows while it sits:** your balance goes up on its own, so there's nothing to claim.
- **Take it out anytime:** there's no lock-up and no withdrawal fee.
- The rate shown (**% APY**) is what you earn after fees. It changes over time with demand for borrowing.

## Good to know

- Savings is a crypto yield product, not a bank account. It isn't FDIC-insured, and like any lending protocol, it carries smart-contract and borrower risk.
- Your main screen shows how much you've **Earned** so far, plus your Savings **Activity**.
- If you see **Not enough ETH on Base for gas**, add a small amount of ETH on Base to cover the network fee.
