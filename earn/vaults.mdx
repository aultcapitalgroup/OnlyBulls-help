Vaults are being rolled out gradually. If you don't see **Earn** in the More menu, they aren't available on your account yet.

**Earn** lists yield vaults run by third-party protocols: **Morpho** on Ethereum and Base, and **Kamino** on Solana. A vault lends your deposit to vetted borrowers and passes the interest on to you. Rates change over time.

## Find a vault

Open **More → Earn**. You can:

- Search for an asset or vault
- Filter by network
- Sort by APY, TVL (total value locked), popularity, earnings, or name

New or higher-risk vaults are hidden by default. To see them, turn on **Include new or high-risk vaults**.

Tap a vault to see how it works, its **Total Value Locked**, **Share Price**, **Performance Fee**, **Curator**, and an estimate of what you'd earn per \$1,000 deposited.

## Withdraw

Open the vault and tap **Withdraw** to take out a specific amount, or **Redeem all shares** to exit completely. (Kamino vaults on Solana don't support Redeem, so use Withdraw instead.)

<Warning>
**Third-party risk**

Vaults are run by independent protocols, not by OnlyBulls. They can lose value through smart-contract bugs, borrower defaults, or market events. Only deposit what you're comfortable putting at risk.
</Warning>
