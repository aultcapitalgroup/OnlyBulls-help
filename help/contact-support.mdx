Can't find your answer here? We're happy to help.

- **Email:** [support@onlybulls.com](mailto:support@onlybulls.com)
- **Contact form:** in the web app, click the **?** icon in the header or the **Support** link in the footer. You can also use the [contact form on our website](https://onlybulls.com/support). Fill in your name, email, and message, then click **Send Message**.

<Card title="Send us an email" icon="envelope" href="mailto:support@onlybulls.com">
  support@onlybulls.com
</Card>

We reply by email.

## What to include

You'll get a faster answer if you tell us:

- The email address on your OnlyBulls account
- Your device and whether you're using iOS, Android, or the web
- What you were trying to do, and any error message you saw (a screenshot helps)
- For wallet issues: the asset, the network, the amount, and the **transaction hash** if you have one. You can find it in your [wallet activity](/wallet/activity).

<Warning>
**We'll never ask for your login or keys**

OnlyBulls support will never ask for your password, sign-in links, verification codes, or private key. Don't share them with anyone who contacts you claiming to be from OnlyBulls.
</Warning>

## Suspended accounts

If your account is suspended, you'll be signed out and see **This account has been suspended.** If you try to sign in again, you'll see **This account has been disabled**.

Accounts are suspended by our team, usually for breaking the [community guidelines](/social/community-guidelines) or our [Terms of Service](https://onlybulls.com/terms).

If you think it's a mistake, tap **Contact support** on the suspension screen. That opens an email to support@onlybulls.com with the subject "Suspended account appeal". You can also send that email yourself.
