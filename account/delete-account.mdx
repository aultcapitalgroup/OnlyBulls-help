<Danger>
**Move your funds first**

Deleting your account is permanent. Your wallet is tied to your OnlyBulls login, so **before you delete your account, move any crypto or cash out of your wallet.** You can [send it to another wallet](/wallet/send-and-receive) or [export your private key](/wallet/wallet-security#export-your-private-key).
</Danger>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your profile and tap Account">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Scroll down to Danger Zone and tap Delete Account">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm by tapping Delete">
  </Step>
  <Step title="If you haven't signed in recently, you'll be asked to Confirm it's you">
    Sign in again with your password, Google, or Apple.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your account and data are deleted, and you're returned to the welcome screen.

## If deletion fails

If you see **Failed to delete account. Please try again.**, a swap or staking transaction may still be processing. Wait a few minutes and try again.

If you're browsing the web app as a guest, sign out and back in, then try again.

If it keeps failing, [contact support](/help/contact-support).
