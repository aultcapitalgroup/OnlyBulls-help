## Open your profile

- **iOS and Android:** tap the person icon in the top-right corner of the **Wallet** tab. The avatar in the **Social** and **Explore** headers opens it too.
- **Web:** click your avatar in the top-right corner.

Your profile shows:

- Your name and **@display name**. Tap to copy it.
- Your wallet address. Tap to copy it.
- Your daily streak.
- **Agent Personality**: a slider from **Professional** to **Playful** that sets the tone of the AI assistant.
- Your achievements.
- A **Settings** list:
  - **Account**
  - **Manage alerts**
  - **Notifications**
  - **Sync Contacts**
  - **Bookmarks** (saved news articles)
  - **Wallet settings**

We give you a random display name when you sign up, so change it to whatever you like. Display names are checked against our community rules. If yours is rejected, you'll see one of these messages:

- **This display name is already taken.**
- **This display name is reserved and cannot be used.**
- **Please choose a display name with valid characters.**

## Change your language

Go to **Profile → Account → Language** and choose **English** or **Português (Brasil)**.

The first time you open the app, it uses your device's language. The language setting applies to the device you change it on, not to your whole account.

## Change your password

Go to **Profile → Account** and find **Account Security**.

- **Email accounts:** tap **Change password by email**. We'll email you a link to set a new password.
- **Google or Apple accounts:** the password row says **Managed by Google** or **Managed by Apple**. Change your password with that provider.
