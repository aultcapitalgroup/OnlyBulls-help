## Notification settings

- **iOS and Android:** open your profile and tap **Notifications**.
- **Web:** click the bell icon, then the gear icon.

You can turn each type of notification on or off:

| Setting | What it covers |
| --- | --- |
| **Daily Briefs** | Your morning and evening market briefs. See [Briefs](/ai/briefs). |
| **Price Alerts** | Alerts you've set when they trigger. See [Price alerts](/markets/price-alerts). |
| **Market Pulse** | A midday snapshot of your watchlists. |
| **Wallet Activity** | Sends, receives, and failed transactions in your wallet. |
| **Content Moderation** | Updates when something you posted is moderated. |

To take a break from all of them at once, turn on **Pause Notifications**.

<Note>
**Allow notifications on your phone**

OnlyBulls can only send push notifications if your phone allows it. If you're not getting any, open your phone's **Settings → Notifications → OnlyBulls** and make sure they're turned on.
</Note>

On the web you can't turn on push notifications. You'll see **Turn on push notifications in the OnlyBulls mobile app.**

## Your notification inbox

Tap the bell icon to see your recent notifications. Tap one to open it.

Use the filters at the top to show **All**, **Price alerts**, **Activity** (wallet and moderation updates), or **News & offers** (briefs and Market Pulse). Swipe a notification to delete it.

The **…** menu at the top of the inbox has:

- **Snooze notifications**: pause them for 30 minutes, 1 hour, 8 hours, or 24 hours. Choose **Unsnooze** to turn them back on early.
- **Mark all as read**
- **Delete all**: on mobile you have a few seconds to undo, and on the web you'll be asked to confirm first.

On iOS and Android you can also reach the inbox from **More → Notifications**.
