Watchlists let you group the tokens you want to keep an eye on. They also shape your [Market Pulse](/account/notifications), [morning brief](/ai/briefs), and AI answers.

Open them from **Explore → Watchlists → View all**, or **More → Watchlists**.

<Note>
**Crypto only**

Watchlists hold crypto tokens, including onchain stocks. Regular stocks and forex pairs can't be added.
</Note>

## Create a watchlist

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap New watchlist">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Enter a **Watchlist name** (up to 40 characters) and, if you like, a short description.
    Tap **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your tokens">
    Search by name, ticker, or contract address, and tick the tokens you want.
    You can also pick from **Suggested for you**, **Trending**, or **Biggest dips**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Add symbols">
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can have up to **20 watchlists**, and each one needs a different name.

## Add a token from its page

On a token's page, tap the **star**. Choose a watchlist, or create a new one.

The same token on two different networks counts as two separate items.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Import, then Take a photo or Choose screenshots">

  <Step title="OnlyBulls scans the images and lists the tokens it found, up to 25">

  <Step title="Review the list">
    Fix anything marked **Invalid ticker** or **Did you mean…?**, and remove anything you don't want.

  </Step>
</Steps>

Screenshot import uses AI, so you'll need to have agreed to AI features. It only picks up crypto tokens.

## Manage a watchlist

- **Remove a token:** swipe left on it and tap **Delete**. You can **Undo** for a few seconds.
- **Reorder tokens:** press and hold a token, then drag it.
- **Move or copy a token** to another list: tap **⋯** on the token.
- **Rename, duplicate, or delete a list:** tap **Manage** in the watchlist, or **⋯** on the list in the overview. When you delete a list, you can **Undo** for a few seconds.
- **Reorder lists:** press and hold a list, or drag its handle.

## Share a watchlist

Open a watchlist and tap **Share**. You can **Share Image**, which includes a QR code, or **Copy Link**. When someone opens your link, they get their own copy of the list.

## Trending lists

The **Trending** tab shows watchlists curated by OnlyBulls AI. Open one and tap **Copy to my watchlists** to make it your own.

## AI insights for a watchlist

Inside a watchlist, tap **Try Agent Intelligence** for an AI summary of how your tokens are doing. See [AI insights](/ai/ai-insights).
