title: Asset pages and charts
description: What you'll find on crypto, stock, and forex pages, how to use the chart, and what "Happening Now" means.
---

Tap any asset to open its page. What you see depends on the type of asset.

## Crypto tokens

Crypto pages show:

- A live price and chart
- **Your Holdings**, if you own it
- **Key Statistics**: 24h change, market cap, and 24h volume
- **About**: the network and contract address
- **Happening Now**: an AI summary of what's moving the price
- News and community posts

At the top, tap the **bell** to set a [price alert](/markets/price-alerts) or the **star** to add the token to a [watchlist](/markets/watchlists).

At the bottom (iOS and Android), **Transfer** lets you send or receive, and **Buy & sell** lets you buy, sell, or swap. See [Activity and token details](/wallet/activity#token-details).

## Stocks

Stock pages are for research. You can't buy or sell regular stocks in OnlyBulls. They show:

- Price, chart, and the day's dollar volume, plus pre-market and after-hours prices when they apply
- **Key Statistics**: open, high, low, volume, **Next Earnings**, market cap, shares outstanding, and short interest
- **About**: industry, exchange, headquarters, and employees
- **Financials**: annual or quarterly
- **You May Also Like**: related companies
- **Happening Now**, news, and community posts

Watchlists and price alerts are for crypto only, so stock pages don't have the bell or star.

## Forex

Forex pages show the price, chart, open, bid, ask, high, low, and the currency pair, plus Happening Now and community posts.

## Using the chart

- Choose a period: **1D**, **1W**, **1M**, **1Y**, **YTD**, or **All**.
- Switch between a line and a candlestick chart.
- For stocks on the 1D view, turn on **Ext hours** to include pre-market and after-hours trading.
- Tap the expand icon to open the chart full screen.

## Happening Now

**Happening Now** is a short AI-written summary of recent activity for the asset, labelled with how long ago it was written. Tap **Try Agent Intelligence** to ask the [AI assistant](/ai/ai-chat) about it.

Happening Now only appears if you've agreed to use AI features. AI summaries can be wrong and aren't financial advice.
