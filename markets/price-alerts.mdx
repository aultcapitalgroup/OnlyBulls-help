Price alerts notify you when a crypto token **rises above** or **falls below** a price you choose.

## Create an alert

<Steps>
  <Step title="Do one of these">
    - Tap the **bell** on a token's page.
    - Open **Explore → Alerts**, or your profile's **Manage alerts**, and tap **Create new alert**.
    - Press and hold a token in a list and choose **Set price alert**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your target price">
    You'll see how far above or below the current price it is. Shortcut chips (**-10%**, **-5%**, **+5%**, **+10%**) fill in a target for you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how often it fires">
    - **Once**: the alert turns off after it triggers.
    - **Every time** (turn on **Recurring**): the alert keeps watching, and notifies you at most once an hour.
  </Step>
  <Step title="If you like, tick Add to watchlist">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Set price alert">
  </Step>
</Steps>

Whether it's an "above" or "below" alert is set automatically, based on whether your target is higher or lower than the current price.

You can also ask the [AI assistant](/ai/ai-chat) to set an alert for you, for example: *"Alert me if ETH drops below \$3,000."*

## Make sure you get notified

Alerts arrive as **push notifications**, even when OnlyBulls is closed. The first time you create one, tap **Turn on notifications**. If push is off, triggered alerts still appear in your [notification inbox](/account/notifications#your-notification-inbox).

## Manage your alerts

Open **Explore → Alerts → View all**, or **Profile → Manage alerts**. Alerts are grouped by token.

- Tap **⋯** on an alert to **Edit**, **Pause**, **Turn on**, or **Delete** it.
- Swipe left to delete. You can **Undo** for a few seconds.
- Triggered one-time alerts move to **Triggered**. Tap **Set again** to reuse one.

## Limits and rules

- You can have up to **50 alerts**.
- Alerts work for **crypto tokens** that have live price data. Regular stocks, forex, and indices aren't supported.
- You can't have two alerts at the same price on the same token.

## Alert messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Alert limit reached.** | You have 50 alerts. Delete some to add more. |
| **You already have an alert at this price** | Pick a different target. |
| **…is already above/below that price** | Your target has already been passed. Enter a new one. |
| **We can't track this token yet.** | There's no live price for this token, so alerts aren't available. |
| **This alert needs to be recreated** | Delete the alert and create a new one. |
