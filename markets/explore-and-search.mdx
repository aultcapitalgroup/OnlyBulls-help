The **Explore** tab is where you find assets and see what the market is doing.

## What's on Explore

Use the tabs at the top to switch between **All**, **Crypto**, and **Onchain Stocks**.

On **All** you'll find:

- **Popular**: a carousel of widely followed coins.
- **Trending**: the coins getting the most attention right now. Tap **View all** for the full list.
- **Market Movers**: top gainers and losers across **Stocks**, **Crypto**, and **Forex**.
- **Crypto Rankings**: crypto gainers, losers, and highest volume.
- **Watchlists**: your [watchlists](/markets/watchlists), plus trending lists curated by OnlyBulls.
- **Alerts**: your active [price alerts](/markets/price-alerts).
- **More to Explore**: [Economy](/markets/news-and-calendars#economy), [News](/markets/news-and-calendars#news), [Earnings this Week](/markets/news-and-calendars#earnings-calendar), and [Briefs](/ai/briefs).

On US market holidays, a **Market Holiday** banner reminds you that US markets are closed.

<Tip>
**Long-press for shortcuts**

On iOS and Android, press and hold any coin in a list to **Set price alert**, **Add to watchlist**, or open it.
</Tip>

## What are onchain stocks?

**Onchain stocks** are tokenized versions of company shares that live on a blockchain. In OnlyBulls they're for research: you can follow their prices, add them to watchlists, and read about them, but you can't trade them. The same goes for regular stocks and forex pairs.

## Search

Tap the **Search coins and stocks** bar at the top of Explore.

- Type a name or ticker, and results update as you type.
- You can also paste a **contract address** (Ethereum-style, Solana, or TRON) to find an exact token.
- If a token exists on several networks, its result shows **N chains**. Tap to choose the network you want.
- The top result is marked **Best match**.

On iOS and Android, search finds crypto tokens and onchain stocks. On the web, search also finds regular stock and forex tickers.

If you see **Some search sources are unavailable. Showing available results.**, some results may be missing. Try again in a moment.
