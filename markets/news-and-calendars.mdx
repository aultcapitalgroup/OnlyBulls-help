## News

Open **Explore → More to Explore → News** for **Must-Know Headlines**. Your feed is tailored to your watchlists and wallet holdings.

- Tap the search icon to search news by topic or ticker.
- Tap the bookmark icon on any article to save it. Find saved articles under **Profile → Bookmarks**.
- Each article has **Key Points**, the assets it mentions, related articles, and a **Try Agent Intelligence** button for an AI take on the story.

## Market Movers

Open **Explore → Market Movers → View all** to see the biggest **Gainers** and **Losers** in **Stocks**, **Crypto**, or **Forex**. For crypto there's also **High Volume**. Prices may be slightly delayed.

## Earnings calendar

Open **Explore → More to Explore → Earnings this Week**, or tap **Next Earnings** on a stock's page.

- Move between weeks, or tap **Jump to Today**.
- Each company shows the expected **EPS Est**, the **Actual** result once it's reported, and the timing: **Before Open**, **After Close**, **During Market**, or **Time TBD**.

## Economy

Open **Explore → More to Explore → Economy** for key US economic data, updated on weekdays:

- **Jobs**: unemployment rate, labor force participation, and more
- **Prices**: CPI, Core CPI, and PCE
- **Growth**: GDP and industrial production
- **Trade & Global**: trade balance, exports, and imports
- **Monetary Policy**: Fed Funds rate, 10-Year Treasury, and M2
- **Housing & Consumer Sentiment**

Tap any figure to see its chart.

## More market tools on the web

At [onlybulls.com](https://onlybulls.com), the sidebar has a few more research pages:

- **Markets**: movers, high-volume tickers, and trending tickers in one place
- **Trending Tickers**: stocks ranked by social media mentions
- **IPOs**: upcoming, recent, and postponed listings
- **Stock Splits**: past and upcoming splits, with filters
- **Economic Calendar**: upcoming economic releases, filterable by impact, country, and category
