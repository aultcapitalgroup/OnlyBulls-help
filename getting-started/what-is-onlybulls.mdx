## Where to use it

- **iPhone:** [download on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6746166943)
- **Android:** [get it on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.askroi.onlybulls)
- **Web:** [onlybulls.com](https://onlybulls.com)

Your account and wallet are the same everywhere you sign in. A few features are only in the mobile apps; each article says when that's the case.

## What it costs

Creating an account and using the app is free. Some actions have costs:

- **Blockchain network fees** for sending, swapping, and other transactions. OnlyBulls sometimes covers these, and when it does you'll see **Paid by OnlyBulls**.
- **Provider fees** when you buy crypto with a card or bank.
- **Trading fees** on [swaps](/wallet/swap), [Perps](/trading/perps#fees-and-funding), and [Predictions](/trading/predictions).

You'll always see the fees before you confirm.

## Important to know

- **OnlyBulls isn't a bank or a broker.** Your wallet is self-custodial: you control your crypto, and OnlyBulls can't reverse transactions or recover lost keys.
- **Stocks are research-only.** You can follow stocks and onchain stocks, but you can only trade crypto.
- **Some features depend on your country.** Perps and Predictions aren't available everywhere, including in the United States, and card and bank payment options vary by country. See [Availability by country](/help/availability-by-country).
- **Nothing in OnlyBulls is financial advice.** AI answers, briefs, and insights are for information only and can be wrong.
