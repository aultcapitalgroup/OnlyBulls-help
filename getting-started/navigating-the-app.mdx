## The tab bar (iOS and Android)

| Tab | What's there |
| --- | --- |
| **Wallet** | Your balance, holdings, quick actions (**Deposit**, **Swap**, **Send**, **Receive**), and recent activity. See [Your wallet](/wallet/your-wallet). |
| **Social** | The community feed. See [Posting on Social](/social/social-feed). |
| **Trade** (the **+** in the middle) | A menu to **Buy**, **Sell**, **Swap**, **Perps**, **Predictions**, or open **More actions**. |
| **Explore** | Trending coins, market movers, watchlists, alerts, news, and more. See [Explore and search](/markets/explore-and-search). |
| **AI Chat** | Ask askROI anything. See [AI Chat](/ai/ai-chat). |

## Header icons

Most tabs have the same icons at the top:

- **☰ (top left):** opens the **More** menu.
- **Bell:** your [notification inbox](/account/notifications#your-notification-inbox).
- **Person icon:** your [profile and settings](/account/profile-and-settings).
- **QR icon** (Wallet tab): show your wallet address, or scan a code to [connect to an app](/wallet/connect-to-apps).

## The More menu

| Section | Items |
| --- | --- |
| Top | **Swap**, **Buy**, **Sell**, **Scan** |
| **Grow** | **Save**, **Earn**, **Stake**, **Perps** (if available on your account) |
| **Access** | **Discover** (dApps), **Predictions**, **License**, **Connections** (connected apps) |
| **Manage** | **Wallet**, **Wallet settings**, **Alerts**, **Watchlists**, **Notifications** |
| **Coming Soon** | **Stocks** |

Not every item appears for every account. Some features are still rolling out, and **Perps** and **Predictions** aren't available in every country. See [Availability by country](/help/availability-by-country).

## On the web

At [onlybulls.com](https://onlybulls.com), a sidebar on the left replaces the tab bar, with **Home**, **Explore**, **Social**, **Wallet**, and **askROI**. Click your avatar at the top right for your profile and settings, and the **?** icon for help and support.

Some features are only in the mobile apps:

- [Perps](/trading/perps)
- [Contact sync](/account/contact-sync)
- Push notifications
- The wallet unlock with Face ID or fingerprint
- The in-app dApp browser

Private key export is only on the web. See [Wallet security](/wallet/wallet-security#export-your-private-key).
