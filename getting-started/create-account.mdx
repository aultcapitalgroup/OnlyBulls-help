title: Create an account and sign in

---

You can sign in to OnlyBulls with:

- **Email and password**
- **An email sign-in link**, so you don't need a password
- **Google**
- **Apple**

## Sign up

<Tabs>
<Tab title="iOS & Android">

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the app">
    The welcome screen says **Smart wallet, smarter trades**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how to sign up">
    - Tap **Sign up** to use your email address.
    - Tap the **Apple** or **Google** icon to continue with that account. (Apple sign-in also works on Android, where it opens in a browser window.)
  </Step>
  <Step title="If you signed up with email, enter your address and tap Continue">
    Then create a password with **at least 8 characters, one number, and one symbol**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the verification email we send you and tap the link">
    The app notices and moves on by itself once you've verified, so you don't need to come back and tap anything.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish the remaining setup steps">
    See [Your first few minutes](/getting-started/onboarding).
  </Step>
</Steps>

If the verification email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder, then tap **Resend verification email**. You can resend once every 60 seconds.

</Tab>
<Tab title="Web">

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to onlybulls.com">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sign In in the top-right corner">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Continue with Google, Continue with Apple, or Continue with Email">
  </Step>
  <Step title="For email, click Create an account">
    Enter your full name, email, and a password of at least 8 characters, then click **Create account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
**Browsing as a guest**

On the web you can look around before you create an account. Guest data is stored only in your browser. To keep it, open **Account** and use **Link with Apple**, **Link with Google**, or **Link with Email** to turn the guest session into a full account.
</Tip>

</Tab>
</Tabs>

If you see **An account already exists with this email**, you already have an account. Tap **Log in instead**.

If you'd rather not use a password:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Log In, then Email me a sign-in link">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the email from OnlyBulls and tap the link">
    It expires after a few minutes.
  </Step>

  </Step>
</Steps>

If you open the link on a different device or browser from the one you requested it on, you'll be asked to confirm your email address first. That's expected.

## Sign-in error messages

| Message | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Invalid email or password** | The email or password is wrong. Check for typos, or reset your password. |
| **Too many failed attempts. Please try again later.** | Sign-in is temporarily locked after repeated failures. Wait a few minutes. |
| **This link has expired or was already used** | Sign-in links work once and expire quickly. Request a new one. |
| **The email address didn't match this sign-in link** | Enter the same email address you requested the link with. |
| **This account has been disabled** | The account has been suspended. See [Suspended accounts](/help/contact-support#suspended-accounts). |
| **Network error. Please check your connection.** | The app couldn't reach our servers. Check your connection and try again. |

## Sign out

Open your profile (the person icon at the top right of the **Wallet** tab) and tap **Sign out** at the bottom.
