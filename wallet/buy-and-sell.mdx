## Buy crypto

You can buy crypto in two ways:

- **With your Cash balance.** On a token's page, tap **Buy**. If you have enough Cash, OnlyBulls converts your USDC into the token.
- **With a card, bank, or exchange.** Follow the steps below.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Buy">
    Tap the **Trade** button in the middle of the tab bar, then **Buy**. You can also tap **Buy** on a token's page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the token">
    Under **What do you want to buy?**, search for the token. Use the network chips to choose which network it arrives on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount and choose how to pay">
    Payment options are the same as for [depositing cash](/wallet/add-money#payment-methods).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the quote, including the Arrives on network, and tap Continue">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finish paying on the provider's page">
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can buy popular assets such as **BTC, ETH, SOL, USDC, USDT, BNB, AVAX, POL, and TRX**, plus many other tokens on supported networks. Some assets, including **BTC, TRX, and SHIB**, can only be bought in US dollars. They're marked **USD only**.

<Note>
**Depends on your country**

Card, bank, and exchange purchases go through third-party providers, and what they offer depends on your country. If you see **…isn't available to buy in your region**, no provider can sell that token where you are. Try a different token, or buy USDC and [swap](/wallet/swap) it. See [Availability by country](/help/availability-by-country#buying-and-depositing-with-a-card-or-bank).
</Note>

When you sell, your crypto is converted to **Cash (USDC on Base)**.

  <Step title="Enter an amount, or tap Max">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the review screen">
    It shows the network, **Total deducted**, and **Minimum received**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Sell now">
    If the token needs a one-time approval first, the button says **Approve & Sell**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When the sale finishes you'll see **Order complete**, and the proceeds are in your Cash balance.

<Note>
To get your money out to a bank account, send your USDC to an exchange that supports withdrawals to your bank, then withdraw from there. OnlyBulls doesn't pay out directly to bank accounts.
</Note>

## Price impact

For thin markets, a large order can move the price against you. If converting with Cash would cost more than 10% in price impact, OnlyBulls blocks it and shows **Price impact too high**. Try a smaller amount.

## Stocks

You can research stocks and onchain stocks in OnlyBulls, but you can't buy or sell them.
