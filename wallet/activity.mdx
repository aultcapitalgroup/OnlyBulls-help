## Wallet activity

On the **Wallet** tab, scroll to **Recent Activity** and tap **View all** to open **Wallet Activity**.

- **Filter by type:** All, Sent, Received, Traded, Fees, Pending, or Failed.
- **Filter by network:** tap **All networks** and choose one or more networks.
- Transactions are grouped into Today, Yesterday, This Week, This Month, and Older. Tap **Search older activity** at the bottom to load more.

Tap the menu on any transaction for these options:

- **View token details**
- **View on Explorer**: opens the transaction on the blockchain's public explorer.
- **Copy Tx Hash**: copies the transaction ID. Include it when you [contact support](/help/contact-support) about a transaction.

<Tip>
**Pending for a long time?**

Most transactions confirm within a few minutes, but busy networks can take longer. Bitcoin can take an hour or more at the **Slow** speed. Use **View on Explorer** to see its status on the blockchain itself.
</Tip>

## Token details

Tap any token in your holdings to open its page. It shows:

- A price chart
- **Your Holdings**: your balance and its value
- **Key Statistics**: 24h change, market cap, and 24h volume
- **About**: the network, the contract address (tap to copy), and decimals
- **Recent Activity** for this token, plus related news and community posts

At the top you can add the token to a [watchlist](/markets/watchlists), set a [price alert](/markets/price-alerts), or share it.

At the bottom (on iOS and Android):

- **Transfer**: **Receive crypto** or **Send crypto**
- **Buy & sell**: **Buy**, **Sell**, **Swap**, and, where available, **Perps** and **Protect**

Tokens flagged **Spam** or **Suspicious** have these buttons turned off to protect you.
