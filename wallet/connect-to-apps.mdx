title: Connect your wallet to apps
description: Use your OnlyBulls wallet with other crypto apps through WalletConnect or the in-app browser, approve requests safely, and disconnect.

You can use your OnlyBulls wallet with other crypto apps, often called dApps, in two ways:

- **WalletConnect:** scan a QR code shown on a website on your computer.
- **Discover:** open dApps directly inside OnlyBulls.

Connecting lets the app see your wallet address and balances. **It can't move funds or sign anything without your approval.**

## Connect with a QR code (WalletConnect)

<Steps>
  <Step title="On the website, choose WalletConnect as your wallet">
    A QR code appears.
  </Step>
  <Step title="In OnlyBulls, open More → Scan">
    You can also use the QR icon on the Wallet tab and choose **Scan**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Point your camera at the QR code">
    If you have a link that starts with `wc:` instead, tap **Paste connection link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the app's name and the networks it's asking for, then tap Connect">
  </Step>
</Steps>

You'll see **Connected**. Head back to the website on your computer to continue.

WalletConnect works with Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, Polygon, BNB Smart Chain, Avalanche, Robinhood Chain, Ault, and Solana.

## Use the Discover browser

Open **More → Discover** to browse dApps. You can also type a dApp's web address into the bar at the top.

- **iOS and Android:** dApps open in OnlyBulls' built-in browser, already connected to your wallet.
- **Web:** dApps open in a new browser tab.

## Approve or reject requests

When a connected app wants you to do something, OnlyBulls shows a request. Read it carefully before you tap anything.

| Request | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Sign in to…** | Proves you own the wallet. Doesn't move any funds. |
| **Sign message** / **Approve structured data** | Signs data for the app. Some signatures can authorize trades or transfers, so only sign if you understand what it's for. |
| **Approve … spending** | Lets the app spend a token from your wallet. |
| **Send Transaction** | Sends funds or interacts with a contract. Check the amount and the recipient. |

Tap **Reject** if anything looks wrong.

<Danger>
**Warnings mean stop**

If you see **Dapp identity mismatch**, **Network mismatch**, **Account mismatch**, or **Signed data could not be fully verified**, the request may not be what it claims to be. Don't approve a request you didn't start yourself.
</Danger>

## See and disconnect connected apps

Open **More → Connections** to see **Connected apps**. They're grouped into **WalletConnect** and **In-app Browser**.

To disconnect one, tap it and choose **Disconnect**. The app loses access to your wallet, and you can reconnect whenever you like.

## Connection errors

| Message | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **This link has already been used** | WalletConnect codes work only once. Refresh the website to get a new QR code. |
| **This request expired** | Start the connection again from the website. |
| **Network not supported** | The app wants a network OnlyBulls doesn't support. |
| **Create a wallet first** | Finish [setting up your wallet](/wallet/your-wallet#if-your-wallet-is-unavailable). |
| **Insufficient funds for gas** | Add some of the network's token (for example ETH) to pay the network fee. |
