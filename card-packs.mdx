title: "Card Packs"
---

OnlyBulls Rips is a digital experience where real, physical Pokémon, One Piece and Sports trading cards are "ripped" to reveal a single card. That card is then added to your wallet and you can sell it back, keep it in your OnlyBulls wallet or ship it to you (Not yet available).

## What is in a Pack

Each pack contains either a Pokémon, One Piece or Sports trading card of varying rarities. One card per pack.

## How to Open a Pack

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start by funding your wallet with USDC on Base">
    If you do not have enough USDC and try to rip a pack, you will be prompted to get more USDC in the app. See more about funding your wallet here: [Add money to your wallet](/wallet/add-money)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a pack">
    Packs start at \$25 and go up to \$500.

    Tapping a pack will open OnlyBull's **What's Inside** page, which shows you recent pulls and the entire card pool.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap the **Rip** button">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap **Confirm**">
    If you have MFA set up on your OnlyBulls account, you will be asked to authenticate.

    Your payment is processed and your pack will be shown when it is ready.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap **Open pack**">
    Your pack is opened and you are shown the card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How to Sell a Card

After you open a pack, you can choose to **Sell** or **Keep** the card you pulled.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Sell">
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Sale Details">
    You will be shown the **Buyback offer** which will expire within a certain timeframe.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Sell">
    The sale is processed in the background and the USDC will be added back into your wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Card title="Note:" type="danger">
  All card sales are final. Estimated value is an estimate. A buyback offer is the amount you would actually receive, and it expires.
</Card>
