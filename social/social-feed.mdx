The **Social** tab is where OnlyBulls members share what they're watching. It's one feed, with the newest posts first.

## Write a post

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the composer">
    Tap the composer at the top of **Social**: "Share an idea. Use \$ before a ticker."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your post">
    It can be up to 1,500 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag an asset">
    Type `$` and pick the asset from the suggestions, for example `$BTC` or `$AAPL`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tag your outlook (optional)">
    Choose **Bullish**, **Bearish**, or **Neutral**. Tap the chip again to clear it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap Post">
  </Step>
</Steps>

Your post is checked automatically before it appears. You'll see **Submitted for review**, and it shows up once it's approved, usually within moments. Posts that break the [community guidelines](/social/community-guidelines) are removed, and you'll get a notification explaining why.

<Tip>
**Cashtags**

Only cashtags you pick from the suggestion list are tagged. Tagged posts also appear in that asset's community, where other people following it will see them. If **Post** is greyed out, you may still be partway through typing a `$ticker`. Finish it or pick a suggestion.
</Tip>

## Comment and like

- Tap **Comment** on a post to open it and add a comment (up to 500 characters). Comments are also reviewed before they appear.
- Tap the heart to **Like** a post or comment.
- Tap **Share post** to send a link to it.

## Delete your posts and comments

Open your post, tap **⋯**, and choose **Delete**. Deleting a post also removes its comments and likes. To delete a comment, tap **⋯** on your comment.

You can't edit a post after publishing. Delete it and post again instead.

## Asset communities

Every asset has its own discussion:

- Open an asset's page and scroll to **Social**. Tap **View all** to see every post, or **Share your take** to post about it.
- Or, on the **Social** tab, tap the search icon and search for a ticker or \$cashtag.

## Who can post

Anyone with an OnlyBulls account can post, comment, and like. On the web, if you're browsing as a guest, you can read and like but you'll need to sign in to post or comment.

To keep the feed healthy, there are limits on how quickly you can post, comment, and like. If you see **Slow down**, wait a moment and try again.

Your display name and photo appear on your posts. Change them in [your profile](/account/profile-and-settings#edit-your-profile).
