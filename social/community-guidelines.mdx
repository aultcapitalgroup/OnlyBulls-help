OnlyBulls Social works best when it's useful, honest, and respectful. By posting, you agree to these rules.

## The rules

1. **Be respectful.** No hate speech, slurs, harassment, insults aimed at a person, or threats.
2. **No spam.** No repetitive promotion, "join my Telegram" links, or paid signal groups.
3. **Keep it honest.** No scams or manipulation: no "guaranteed returns," pump-and-dump schemes, fake giveaways, or impersonation. Never ask anyone for a seed phrase or private key.
4. **Keep it appropriate.** No sexual content or content about self-harm. Keep politics off cashtags.
5. **No undisclosed bots.** Automated accounts aren't allowed to pose as real people.

Strong opinions are welcome, whether bullish or bearish, and so is the occasional swear word about the markets.

## How moderation works

Every post and comment is checked automatically before it's published. Until it's approved, nobody can see it, including you.

If something you wrote is removed, you'll get a notification such as **Your post was removed**, with the reason. You can turn these notices off under **Profile → Notifications → Content Moderation**.

Display names are checked too. A name that breaks the rules is changed back to your previous one.

Accounts that repeatedly break the rules can be [suspended](/help/contact-support#suspended-accounts).

## Report a post or a person

If you see something that breaks these rules, email [support@onlybulls.com](mailto:support@onlybulls.com) with a link to the post. (Tap **⋯ → Copy link** on the post.)

<Warning>
**Watch out for scams**

Nobody from OnlyBulls will message you offering investment returns, giveaways, or "account recovery." Never share your password, sign-in links, codes, or private key.
</Warning>
